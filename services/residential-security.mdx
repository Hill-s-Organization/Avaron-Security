## How Residential Security Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Free Consultation">
    Our security consultant visits your property or meets virtually to assess entry points, patrol routes, and security vulnerabilities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Custom Security Plan">
    We prepare a security plan specific to your community or estate — including staffing levels, shift schedules, and emergency protocols.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Officer Assignment & Briefing">
    Officers are assigned to your property and fully briefed on your community rules, resident roster, and special instructions before day one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ongoing Management">
    Your account manager is available for scheduling changes, incidents, or adjustments to the program at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Ideal For

- **Gated communities and HOAs** — regular patrol and gate management with resident communication
- **Luxury apartment complexes** — lobby security, parking enforcement, and after-hours coverage
- **Private estates** — 24/7 dedicated officer presence with full grounds monitoring
- **Vacation properties** — scheduled check-ins and patrol during your absence
- **Short-term events** — parties, family gatherings, or estate sales requiring temporary security

## Officer Conduct Standards

Residential officers are trained to balance security with discretion. They are professional, courteous, and mindful of the residential nature of the environment — firm when security requires it, but always respectful of residents' privacy and comfort.
