## Corporate Security Solutions

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Lobby & Reception">
    Professional lobby officers serve as the first point of contact for your building, providing:
    - Visitor sign-in and credential verification
    - Tenant and employee access management
    - Package and delivery receipt
    - Enforcement of building access policies
    - Emergency first responder coordination
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Patrol Services">
    Uniformed patrol officers conduct regular rounds across your property to:
    - Deter theft, vandalism, and trespass
    - Monitor parking lots and garages
    - Check doors, locks, and emergency exits
    - Respond to alarms and suspicious activity
    - Document and report all incidents
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Loss Prevention">
    Specialized loss prevention officers are deployed for retail, warehouse, and distribution environments:
    - Overt and plainclothes floor presence
    - Inventory shrinkage monitoring
    - Employee theft deterrence
    - Vendor and contractor oversight
    - Incident documentation and prosecution support
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Executive Protection">
    For C-suite executives or high-profile personnel, Avaron provides:
    - Personal protection officers (PPO)
    - Secure transport and escort services
    - Advance security sweeps for off-site meetings
    - Residential and travel security planning
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Contract Structures

| Contract Type | Description |
|---|---|
| Full-time dedicated officer | One or more officers assigned exclusively to your site on a fixed schedule |
| Part-time / shift coverage | Officers scheduled for specific shifts — evenings, overnights, weekends |
| Roving patrol | Officers patrol multiple sites in a defined geographic area |
| On-call coverage | Officers available for scheduled special events or surge coverage |
