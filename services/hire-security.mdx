## Types of Security Officers Available

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Unarmed Officers">
    Unarmed security officers are ideal for most commercial, event, and residential environments. They provide:
    - Access control and visitor management
    - Patrol of premises and parking areas
    - Customer service and incident de-escalation
    - Monitoring of CCTV and alarm systems
    - Detailed incident report writing
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Armed Officers">
    Armed security officers hold additional state-issued firearms permits and are suited for higher-risk environments. They are deployed for:
    - Cash or valuables transport escorts
    - High-value asset protection
    - Executive and VIP personal protection
    - Facilities with elevated threat profiles

    <Note>Armed officer assignments are subject to a site assessment and additional contract terms.</Note>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Plainclothes Officers">
    Plainclothes officers provide discreet security presence without uniformed visibility. Common uses include:
    - Loss prevention in retail environments
    - Undercover event crowd monitoring
    - Executive protection in public settings
    - Corporate investigations support
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
