## Event Security Services

### Access Control & Credential Verification
Our officers manage entry points with precision — checking tickets, verifying credentials, enforcing capacity limits, and preventing unauthorized access.

### Crowd Management
Trained in crowd dynamics and emergency egress, our teams ensure safe movement of guests throughout your venue — including during high-pressure situations.

### VIP & Executive Protection
Discreet, professional escort and close-protection services for keynote speakers, performers, executives, and special guests.

### Perimeter Security
Officers patrol the perimeter of outdoor venues and internal zones to deter trespass, theft, and unauthorized access.

### Emergency Response Coordination
Our on-site supervisors coordinate directly with venue staff, local law enforcement, and emergency services, ensuring a rapid, organized response to any incident.

## Planning Your Event Security

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pre-Event Consultation">
    Our team reviews your event plan, venue layout, expected attendance, and risk factors to recommend the right staffing configuration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Security Plan Development">
    We prepare a written security plan covering post assignments, communication protocols, emergency procedures, and escalation contacts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Officer Briefing">
    All officers are briefed on event-specific orders before deployment — including venue rules, VIP protocols, and communication channels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="On-Site Supervision">
    A dedicated Avaron supervisor is on-site throughout the event to manage officer deployment, handle incidents, and coordinate with your team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post-Event Report">
    Within 24 hours of event completion, you receive a written incident report and activity summary.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Avaron Security recommends scheduling your event security team at least two weeks in advance for large events. For events over 500 attendees, a site walk is required.
</Note>
