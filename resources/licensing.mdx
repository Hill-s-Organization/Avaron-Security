Avaron Security operates in full compliance with all applicable state and local regulations governing the private security industry. Our licensing, insurance, and certification standards exceed minimum requirements — giving you confidence that every officer we deploy meets the highest professional and legal standards.

## Licensing & Registration

Avaron Security holds all required state licenses to operate as a private security contractor. Our company license is renewed annually and remains in good standing with the applicable state regulatory authority.

  <Card title="Company License" icon="building-shield">

  <Card title="Officer Licensing" icon="id-badge">

## Officer Certifications

All Avaron Security officers complete the following before their first assignment:

| Certification | Details |
|---|---|
| State Security Officer License | Current, active license issued by the applicable state regulatory agency |
| Certified Security Officer Training | Minimum 40-hour pre-assignment training program |
| Background Investigation | Comprehensive criminal history, identity, and employment check |
| First Aid / CPR | Current certification for emergency medical response |
| Use of Force Training | Annual training on force continuum, de-escalation, and legal standards |

## Insurance Coverage

Avaron Security maintains the following insurance policies on behalf of all clients:

- **Commercial General Liability** — Covers third-party bodily injury and property damage arising from our operations
- **Workers' Compensation** — Covers all Avaron officers for work-related injuries
- **Professional Liability (E&O)** — Covers claims arising from errors or omissions in our security services
- **Umbrella / Excess Liability** — Provides additional limits above primary policies

<Note>
  Certificates of insurance are available at no charge upon request. Contact your account manager or email our office to receive a certificate for your records.
</Note>

## Fingerprinting Compliance

Avaron Security's fingerprinting technicians are certified by the applicable state authority to operate live scan fingerprinting equipment and submit prints to authorized agencies. We comply fully with state biometric data regulations — your fingerprint data is transmitted to the requesting agency and not retained by Avaron Security.
