Avaron Security is committed to protecting the privacy of every individual we serve. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your data.

<Note>
  This privacy policy was last updated and is effective as of the current publication date. If you have questions, contact us at [james.avaronsecurity@gmail.com](mailto:james.avaronsecurity@gmail.com).
</Note>

## Information We Collect

### From Clients & Prospective Clients
- Contact information (name, email address, phone number, business name)
- Service requirements and location information provided during quote requests or consultations
- Billing and payment information for service contracts
- Communication records (emails, call logs)

### From Fingerprinting Service Users
- Government-issued ID information required to verify identity
- Fingerprint images captured during the appointment (**transmitted directly to the requesting agency; not retained by Avaron Security**)
- Appointment scheduling information

### Automatically Collected Information
- Website usage data (pages visited, time spent, browser type) via standard web analytics tools

## How We Use Your Information

Avaron Security uses personal information solely to:

- Prepare and deliver security service quotes and proposals
- Schedule and staff security officers for your engagement
- Process fingerprinting appointments and submit prints to authorized agencies
- Communicate with you about your service, account, or appointment
- Process payments and manage billing
- Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

**We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.**

## Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Client contact and contract records | Duration of service relationship + 7 years |
| Fingerprint images | Transmitted and deleted immediately upon submission |
| Billing records | 7 years per standard accounting requirements |
| Website analytics | 26 months rolling |

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** the personal information Avaron Security holds about you
- **Correct** inaccurate or outdated information
- **Request deletion** of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements
- **Opt out** of non-essential communications

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@avaronsecurity.com.

## Security Measures

Avaron Security uses industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.
