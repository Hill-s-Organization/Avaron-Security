Security emergencies don't follow business hours. Avaron Security's 24/7 operations center is staffed around the clock to handle urgent officer deployment requests, on-site incidents, and shift coverage emergencies.

<Warning>
  If you are experiencing an active emergency involving a threat to life or property, call **911** immediately before contacting Avaron Security.
</Warning>

## When to Use Emergency Support

Contact the Avaron Security emergency operations line when:

- An officer does not report for a scheduled shift and you need immediate replacement
- An on-site incident requires supervisor or additional officer response
- You need same-day or next-hour security coverage
- An active situation on your property requires Avaron operations involvement
- You have an urgent question about an active contract or deployment

## Shift Replacement Guarantee

For clients on active service contracts, Avaron Security guarantees:

- **Response confirmation within 15 minutes** of an emergency call
- **Replacement officer dispatched within 2 hours** of a no-show report
- **On-site supervisor available within 1 hour** for active security incidents

<Note>
  Shift replacement and emergency response guarantees apply to active service contracts. Response times for new, uncontracted clients are subject to officer availability.
</Note>

## What to Tell the Operations Center

When you call the emergency line, have the following ready:

1. **Your account name and contract number** (if applicable)
2. **Site address** where coverage is needed
3. **Nature of the issue** — missed shift, active incident, urgent new coverage
4. **Number of officers needed** and any special requirements
5. **Your callback number**

<Tip>
  First-time clients needing urgent coverage should call the operations line directly. Our team will gather information, assess availability, and provide a quote in real time.
</Tip>
