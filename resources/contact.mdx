## Fingerprinting Appointment Scheduling

To schedule a fingerprinting appointment:

1. Email fingerprinting@avaronsecurity.com with your preferred dates and times
2. Include the name of the requesting agency and your ORI number
3. Our team will confirm your appointment within one business hour during office hours

See the [Fingerprinting Overview](/fingerprinting/overview) for appointment requirements.

<Tip>
  For the fastest response, include as much detail as possible in your initial message — service type, location, dates, and number of officers needed.
</Tip>
