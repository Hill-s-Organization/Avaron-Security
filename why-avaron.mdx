## Our Officer Standards

<Accordion title="Licensing & Certification">
  All Avaron Security officers hold a current, state-issued security officer license before their first assignment. Licenses are verified and renewed on a regular schedule. Officers receive additional site-specific briefings for every new engagement.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Background Checks">
  Every officer candidate undergoes a comprehensive background investigation including criminal history, identity verification, employment history, and reference checks before joining the Avaron team.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Training Program">
  Officers complete a formal training curriculum covering access control, crowd management, emergency procedures, report writing, use-of-force policy, and customer interaction. Continuing education is required annually.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Uniform & Professional Standards">
  Officers deploy in professional uniforms appropriate to the assignment — formal business attire, tactical uniform, or plainclothes, depending on your preference and the threat environment.
</Accordion>
