Have questions about Avaron Security's services? We've compiled answers to the most frequently asked questions from our clients. If you don't find what you're looking for, [contact our team](/resources/contact) — we're happy to help.

## General Questions

<Accordion title="What areas does Avaron Security serve?">
  Avaron Security provides services throughout our licensed service area. Contact our office to confirm coverage for your specific location. We routinely serve clients across a broad regional area and can discuss service availability during your free consultation.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How quickly can security officers be deployed?">
  Most standard assignments can be staffed within 24–48 hours of contract execution. For urgent coverage, our 24/7 operations line can assess same-day availability. See [Emergency Support](/resources/emergency-support) for urgent contact details.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a minimum contract term?">
  For one-time events, there is no ongoing commitment — you pay only for the hours worked. For recurring corporate or residential contracts, a 30-day minimum is standard, with month-to-month and annual options available.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How are your prices determined?">
  Pricing depends on the service type, number of officers, hours of coverage, officer classification (unarmed, armed, plainclothes), and contract duration. [Request a free quote](/get-a-quote) to receive a detailed, itemized proposal.
</Accordion>

## Officer Qualifications

<Accordion title="Are your officers licensed?">
  Yes. All Avaron Security officers hold a current, state-issued security officer license before their first assignment. Licensing documentation is available upon request.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do you perform background checks on your officers?">
  Yes. Every officer undergoes a comprehensive background investigation — including criminal history, identity verification, employment history, and reference checks — before joining the Avaron team.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What training do your officers receive?">
  Officers complete a certified training curriculum covering access control, crowd management, emergency procedures, report writing, use-of-force policy, and customer interaction. Annual continuing education is required.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I request a specific officer for my site?">
  Yes. For ongoing contracts, clients may request that specific officers be assigned to their account. Subject to availability and scheduling, we do our best to honor officer continuity requests.
</Accordion>

## Fingerprinting Questions

<Accordion title="What do I need to bring to a fingerprinting appointment?">
  Bring a valid government-issued photo ID (driver's license, passport, or state ID), the authorization form from the requesting agency, and your ORI number. See the [full requirements list](/fingerprinting/overview#what-to-bring).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How long does a fingerprinting appointment take?">
  Most appointments take 15–20 minutes. Arrival 5 minutes early is recommended to allow for check-in and form review.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="When will I receive my background check results?">
  Results are sent directly to the requesting agency, not to you. Live scan state submissions typically return in 3–5 business days; FBI submissions take 7–10 business days. Contact your employer or licensing board to check the status of your results.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if my fingerprints are rejected?">
  If the receiving agency rejects your prints due to quality issues, Avaron Security will re-fingerprint you at no charge. Bring your original receipt and the rejection notice to your follow-up appointment.
</Accordion>

## Billing & Administration

<Accordion title="What payment methods do you accept?">
  Avaron Security accepts checks, ACH bank transfers, and major credit cards for service contracts. Fingerprinting appointments accept cash, debit, and major credit cards at the time of service.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can you provide a certificate of insurance?">
  Yes. Certificates of insurance are available upon request for all active service contracts. Contact your account manager or email our office.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I request a change to my service?">
  Contact your dedicated account manager directly. Changes to staffing levels, shift hours, or service scope can typically be implemented within 24–48 hours.
</Accordion>
