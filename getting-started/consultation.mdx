## What Happens During a Consultation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Needs Assessment">
    Your consultant will ask about your property type, security concerns, coverage hours, and any previous incidents or vulnerabilities. This takes about 15–20 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Site Review (If Applicable)">
    For larger or more complex engagements — such as corporate campuses, residential communities, or large venues — a physical site walk may be scheduled to assess entry points, patrol routes, and high-risk areas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Service Recommendation">
    Based on the assessment, your consultant will recommend the appropriate officer types, staffing levels, and coverage schedule to meet your security objectives within your budget.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Quote Delivery">
    Within 24 hours of the consultation, you'll receive a written proposal with a full breakdown of services and pricing. There's no obligation to proceed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Consultation Options

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Phone Consultation">
    A 20–30 minute call with a security specialist. Ideal for smaller engagements, events, or individuals who want a quick overview of options and pricing ranges.

    **Best for:** Single-site businesses, one-time events, individual fingerprinting questions, residential inquiries.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Virtual Consultation">
    A video call that allows you to share floor plans, venue layouts, or site photos with your consultant for a more detailed assessment without an in-person visit.

    **Best for:** Multi-location businesses, event venues, residential communities planning new security programs.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="On-Site Assessment">
    A physical walk of your property by an Avaron security consultant. Includes evaluation of access points, lighting, patrol coverage areas, and existing security infrastructure.

    **Best for:** Corporate campuses, gated communities, large venues, high-value asset environments.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## What to Prepare

To make the most of your consultation, have the following ready:

- Address or general location of the property or event
- Approximate square footage or capacity (for venues)
- Current security measures, if any
- Key concerns or recent incidents
- Preferred coverage hours and start date
- Budget range (optional, but helpful)

<Note>
  All consultations are confidential. Information shared during your consultation is used solely to prepare your security proposal and is never shared with third parties.
</Note>
