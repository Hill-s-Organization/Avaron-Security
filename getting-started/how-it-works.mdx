Getting security coverage through Avaron Security is a straightforward, three-step process. From your first contact to the moment officers step on-site, our operations team handles the details so you can focus on your business, event, or home.

## The Avaron Security Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit Your Request">
    Start with a [free quote request](/get-a-quote) or [consultation booking](/getting-started/consultation). Tell us what you need: the type of security, location, dates, and any special requirements.

    **What to include:**
    - Service type (event, corporate, residential, fingerprinting)
    - Location and coverage area
    - Dates and hours of coverage
    - Number of officers (or we can recommend based on your space)
    - Any special requirements (armed officers, plainclothes, VIP protection)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive & Approve Your Custom Plan">
    Within 24 hours, you'll receive a customized security proposal — including officer types, staffing levels, shift structure, and a transparent cost breakdown.

    Review the plan with your account specialist. Modifications are welcome at no charge. Once you're satisfied, sign the service agreement electronically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Officers Deploy">
    Our operations team schedules your officers, issues site-specific post orders, and conducts a pre-deployment briefing. Officers arrive uniformed, prepared, and ready to protect.

    For most engagements, deployment can begin within **24–48 hours** of contract execution.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ongoing Support">
    Your dedicated account manager remains available throughout your engagement for scheduling changes, incident escalations, billing questions, or service adjustments.

    Operations support is available **24/7** for shift emergencies.
  </Step>
</Steps>
