## What Is Live Scan Fingerprinting?

Live scan is a digital method of capturing fingerprints electronically — without ink and paper. Your fingerprints are scanned, converted to a digital image, and transmitted directly to the requesting agency (such as the FBI, DOJ, or a state licensing board). The process is clean, fast, and more accurate than traditional ink-and-roll methods.

## What to Bring

Please bring the following to your appointment:

- **Valid government-issued photo ID** — Driver's license, state ID, passport, or military ID
- **Completed request form** — Provided by the requesting agency (employer, licensing board, court, etc.)
- **ORI number or agency code** — Identifies the receiving agency for your background check
- **Payment** — See current pricing on the [appointment booking page](/getting-started/consultation)

<Warning>
  Avaron Security cannot process your fingerprints without a valid government-issued ID and the required agency authorization form. Please confirm requirements with your requesting agency before your appointment.
</Warning>
