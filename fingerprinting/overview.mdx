## What Is Ink Card Fingerprinting?

Ink card fingerprinting is the traditional method of capturing fingerprints using ink and hard-copy fingerprint cards (such as the FD-258). A trained technician rolls each finger onto the card to produce a clean, legible print set that you then mail or deliver to the requesting agency.

## What to Bring

Please bring the following to your appointment:

- **Valid government-issued photo ID** — Driver's license, state ID, passport, or military ID
- **Completed request form** — Provided by the requesting agency (employer or licensing board)
- **Fingerprint cards** — If your agency requires a specific card format (such as FD-258), bring blank cards supplied by that agency
- **Payment** — Free for Avaron staff members; \$5 flat fee for public applicants

<Warning>
  Avaron Security cannot process your fingerprints without a valid government-issued ID and the required agency authorization form. Please confirm requirements with your requesting agency before your appointment.
</Warning>

## Turnaround Times

Ink cards are handed to you at the end of your appointment for submission to the requesting agency. Total turnaround depends on how you submit the cards and how quickly the receiving agency processes them, typically 2 to 4 weeks by mail.

<Note>
  Turnaround times are controlled by the receiving agency, not Avaron Security. Delays during peak periods may occur.
</Note>
