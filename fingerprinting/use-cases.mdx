Fingerprint-based background checks are required across a wide range of industries, professions, and legal processes. Avaron Security serves individuals from all walks of life who need fast, accurate fingerprinting to meet compliance requirements from employers, state agencies, federal entities, and courts.

## Common Fingerprinting Use Cases

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Employment Background Checks" icon="briefcase">
    Many employers — particularly in healthcare, education, financial services, and security — require fingerprint-based background checks before hire.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Professional Licensing" icon="id-card">
    State licensing boards require fingerprinting for nurses, teachers, real estate agents, insurance professionals, contractors, and many other licensed occupations.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Security Clearances" icon="shield-halved">
    Federal and state security clearances require FBI-channeled fingerprint submissions as part of the vetting process.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Adoption & Foster Care" icon="child">
    Adoption agencies and foster care programs require comprehensive background checks — including fingerprinting — for prospective parents.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Volunteer Programs" icon="hand-holding-heart">
    Schools, nonprofits, and youth-serving organizations often require fingerprinting for volunteers working with children or vulnerable populations.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Immigration & Visa" icon="plane">
    Certain immigration applications, visa renewals, and citizenship processes require FBI background checks via fingerprint submission.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Legal & Court Orders" icon="gavel">
    Courts and attorneys may require fingerprinting for expungements, record sealing, name changes, or as part of legal proceedings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Firearm Purchases & Permits" icon="scale-balanced">
    Some jurisdictions require fingerprinting as part of the firearm purchase or concealed carry permit application process.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Industry-Specific Requirements

<Accordion title="Healthcare">
  Healthcare workers — including nurses, medical assistants, home health aides, and therapists — are routinely required to complete fingerprint background checks through state health departments and licensing boards before employment or licensure renewal.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Education">
  Teachers, school administrators, counselors, and school volunteers are required to complete fingerprint checks through state departments of education. Requirements vary by state — check with your school district or state licensing authority.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Financial Services">
  FINRA, the FDIC, and state banking regulators require fingerprint background checks for securities professionals, bank employees, and mortgage loan originators.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Real Estate">
  Real estate agents and brokers in most states must submit fingerprints as part of the licensing application or renewal process through the state real estate commission.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Security Industry">
  Security officers themselves must complete fingerprint background checks to obtain state security officer licenses — a process Avaron Security is well-positioned to facilitate.
</Accordion>
