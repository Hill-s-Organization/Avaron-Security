Avaron Security provides fingerprinting for two audiences: our own staff and corporate/business clients, and members of the public who need prints for authorized employment, licensing, or background-screening purposes. We do not process fingerprints for legal court orders, firearm purchases or permits, volunteer programs, immigration and visa applications, or adoption and foster care.

## Authorized Public Fingerprinting Purposes

Avaron can provide fingerprinting services for members of the public who need fingerprints for:

- Employment background checks
- Pre-employment screening
- Professional licensing applications
- Occupational licensing
- School and education program requirements
- Childcare or youth-program applications
- Healthcare employment or licensing
- Security or private-security employment
- Government employment applications
- Fire and EMS-related applications
- Law-enforcement-related applications
- Personal background-check purposes, where the applicable system accepts the fingerprint format
- Other authorized background-screening purposes

## Services We Do Not Provide

Avaron Security does not process fingerprints for the following purposes. Please contact a different fingerprinting provider or your requesting agency directly if your need falls into any of these categories:

- Legal or court-ordered fingerprinting
- Firearm purchases or concealed carry permits
- Volunteer program requirements
- Immigration and visa applications
- Adoption or foster care documentation

## Industry-Specific Requirements

<Accordion title="Healthcare">
  Healthcare workers — including nurses, medical assistants, home health aides, and therapists — are routinely required to complete fingerprint background checks through state health departments and licensing boards before employment or licensure renewal.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Education">
  Teachers, school administrators, counselors, and childcare staff are required to complete fingerprint checks through state departments of education. Requirements vary by state — check with your school district or state licensing authority. Most people are required to go through a local police department.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Financial Services">
  FINRA, the FDIC, and state banking regulators require fingerprint background checks for securities professionals, bank employees, and mortgage loan originators.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Real Estate">
  Real estate agents and brokers in most states must submit fingerprints as part of the licensing application or renewal process through the state real estate commission.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Security Industry">
  Security officers must complete fingerprint background checks to obtain state security officer licenses — a process Avaron Security is well-positioned to facilitate for both our own staff and outside applicants.
</Accordion>

## Before Your Appointment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm Your Agency's Requirements">
    Contact your employer or licensing board to confirm the ORI number, required authorization forms, and any specific instructions for your submission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Gather Your Documents">
    Bring a valid government-issued photo ID, your completed authorization form, and your ORI number. See the [full document checklist](/fingerprinting/overview#what-to-bring).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule Your Appointment">
    [Book your appointment](/getting-started/consultation) at a time that works for you. Most results are returned to the requesting agency within 3 to 10 business days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Not sure if your purpose qualifies? Contact our office before booking and we'll confirm whether we can process your submission.
</Note>
