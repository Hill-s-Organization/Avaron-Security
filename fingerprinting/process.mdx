The Avaron Security fingerprinting process is designed to be quick, comfortable, and stress-free. Most appointments take 15–20 minutes from check-in to completion. Here is exactly what you can expect from the moment you book to the moment your prints are submitted.

## Step-by-Step Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Book Your Appointment">
    Schedule your appointment online, by phone, or in person. When booking, have your ORI number (or agency code) and the name of the requesting organization ready. Confirm you have your required ID and authorization form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arrive & Check In">
    Arrive 5 minutes before your scheduled time. Present your valid government-issued photo ID at the front desk. Our staff will verify your identity and confirm the receiving agency details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Required Forms">
    If you have not already completed the required authorization form from the requesting agency, our staff can assist you. Some agencies provide forms you must complete in advance, so check with your employer or licensing board.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fingerprint Capture">
    A certified fingerprint technician will guide you through the scan. Your fingers are placed one at a time on the live scan device. The process is completely ink-free and takes approximately 5–10 minutes. If a print quality check fails, the technician will re-scan the affected finger — this is common and not a cause for concern.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review & Submission">
    The technician reviews the captured prints for quality before submission. Once approved, the digital file is transmitted directly to the requesting agency. You will receive a transaction number as confirmation of submission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Your Results">
    Results are returned directly to the requesting agency, not to you personally. Contact your employer or licensing board to follow up on your background check status. For FBI submissions, allow 7 to 10 business days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips for Best Results

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Keep Hands Clean & Dry" icon="hands">
    Wash your hands before your appointment but avoid applying lotion — oils and moisture affect scan quality.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Avoid Physical Labor" icon="hand">
    Avoid heavy manual work immediately before your appointment, as worn or roughened fingertips can reduce scan quality.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bring All Documents" icon="folder-open">
    Have your ID, authorization form, and ORI number ready before you arrive to avoid delays.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Arrive Rested" icon="clock">
    Nervous or tense fingers can affect quality. Take a moment to relax — the process is quick and painless.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion title="What if my fingerprints are rejected?">
  Occasionally, poor print quality causes an agency to reject a submission. If this happens, Avaron Security will re-fingerprint you at no additional charge. Bring your original receipt and rejection notice to your follow-up appointment.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I walk in without an appointment?">
  Walk-ins are welcome based on availability, but an appointment guarantees your slot and minimizes wait time. We recommend booking in advance whenever possible.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What forms of payment are accepted?">
  Fingerprinting is free for Avaron staff members. Public applicants pay a flat \$5 fee at the appointment. We accept cash, major credit cards, and debit cards.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Will my fingerprints be stored?">
  Avaron Security transmits your fingerprints directly to the requesting agency and does not retain your biometric data after submission. For storage and retention policies of the receiving agency, consult that agency directly.
</Accordion>

<Note>
  Need to reschedule? Contact us at least 24 hours before your appointment to avoid a missed-appointment fee.
</Note>
