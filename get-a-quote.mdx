## What to Include in Your Request

To help us prepare the most accurate quote, please include as much of the following information as possible:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Service Type">
    Tell us what kind of security you need: event security, corporate/office security, residential security, personal protection, or fingerprinting services.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Location & Dates">
    Provide the address or general location, along with the start date, end date, and daily hours of coverage required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Number of Officers">
    Share your estimate of how many officers you need, or describe the space and we'll recommend appropriate staffing levels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Special Requirements">
    Note any specific needs: armed vs. unarmed officers, uniform type, access control systems, patrol routes, or VIP protection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion title="How long does it take to receive a quote?">
  Most quotes are delivered within 24 business hours of receiving your request. For complex, multi-site, or large-scale deployments, a brief consultation call may be needed before the proposal is finalized.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is the quote free and non-binding?">
  Yes. Avaron Security provides free, no-obligation quotes for all services. There is no cost to request a proposal, and you are under no obligation to proceed.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens after I accept a quote?">
  Once you approve a quote, Avaron Security will send a service agreement for your signature. Upon execution, our operations team begins officer scheduling and site briefings immediately.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I modify the scope after receiving a quote?">
